---
name: writing-text
description: Use when writing or editing text, including responses, documentation, agent instructions, and skills.
---

Keep text concise and direct. Remove unnecessary prose and redundancy while preserving all information, requirements, constraints, and intended behavior. Preserve meaningful qualifiers such as must, only, and unless.

Prefer familiar words and concrete statements. Remove filler introductions, inflated wording, vague praise, and repeated conclusions. Express uncertainty precisely.

Prefer periods and commas over em dashes and semicolons when they express the meaning clearly. Use straight quotation marks (`"`) and apostrophes (`'`) in prose. Preserve punctuation in code, exact quotations, and required syntax.

Use headings, lists, and tables only when they make the content easier to follow. Use a list when a sentence pairs several cases with what to do in each, or when one bullet holds several separate rules.

Fix typos and grammar errors.

Simplify larger sections where needed, resolving inconsistencies and contradictions. Ask the user for clarification when necessary.

Before finishing, check the text against [tells.md](tells.md) and remove the patterns it lists.
