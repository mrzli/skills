# Twelve mixed capitals identified with one spelling slip

The user identified all capitals for Kyrgyzstan, Cambodia, Romania, Bhutan, Laos, Lebanon, Turkmenistan, Mongolia, Estonia, Nepal, Myanmar, and Kazakhstan. Eleven were spelled correctly; “phnop penh” was interpreted as Phnom Penh and corrected. Total coverage remains 79 pairs. Revisit Phnom Penh spelling during later retrieval.
