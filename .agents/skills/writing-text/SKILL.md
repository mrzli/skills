---
name: writing-text
description: Use when writing or editing text, including responses, documentation, agent instructions, and skills.
---

Keep text concise and direct. Remove unnecessary prose and redundancy while preserving all information, requirements, constraints, and intended behavior.

Prefer familiar words and concrete statements. Remove filler introductions, inflated wording, vague praise, forced contrasts, and repeated conclusions. Express uncertainty precisely.

Prefer periods and commas over em dashes and semicolons when they express the meaning clearly. Preserve punctuation in code, exact quotations, and required syntax.

Use headings, lists, and tables only when they make the content easier to follow. Preserve meaningful qualifiers such as must, only, and unless.
